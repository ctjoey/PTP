How to play

Follow a live game with Pick the Play open. Before each snap a play opens, and you have {{ window_seconds }} seconds to call it:

  1. Run or pass?
  2. Left, middle or right? Left and right are as the quarterback looks downfield (the offense's left and right), not the TV picture's.
  3. How far? Total yards gained on the play: Short (5 yards or less), Medium (6-10 yards) or Long (11 or more). An incomplete pass or no gain counts as 0 yards, so it's Short.

You can change your pick until the timer runs out. Then predictions lock, the play runs, and everyone sees the result and the updated leaderboard at the same moment.

For example, you pick Run, Left, Short and it's a run to the left for 7 yards (Medium): that's {{ scoring.type + scoring.direction }} points. Pick Run, Left, Medium and you get all three plus the bonus: {{ scoring.exact }}. A loss of yards scores no distance points (and so no bonus), so a sack or a run stopped behind the line can earn at most {{ scoring.type + scoring.direction }}.

The live leaderboard ranks points in the current game. Your season points add up across every game. Plays cancelled by a penalty or no-play score nothing for anyone.

What counts as left, middle or right, which plays are called, and how official calls are made: see the Rules of the Game.

Head-to-head lounges

Play the same live game against friends with your own private leaderboard.

  • Create a lounge and give it a name. You get a 4-digit code to share.
  • Join a lounge by entering a friend's 4-digit code.
  • A lounge holds up to {{ max_lounge_members }} players. Anyone with the code can join, so only share it with people you want to play with.

Names

Usernames and lounge names are shown to other players. Names that are already taken, or that are offensive, are turned down, so just choose a different one.

Deleting your account

In the iPhone app, go to Settings > Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and permanently, including your username, picks and points, your lounge memberships, and any lounges you host. This can't be undone. You can start again any time with a new username.

Playing in Safari or from a Home Screen icon instead of the iPhone app? Ask the person running your game to remove your account.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.

Troubleshooting

  • "Too late" when picking: the {{ window_seconds }}-second window had closed. Picks open again for the next play.
  • Nothing happening: the game updates live, so check your internet connection. Between plays you'll see "Waiting for the next play".
  • New phone or reinstalled the app: there's no password or email, so your sign-in stays on the device you used. If it's gone, pick a new username.
