PICK THE PLAY LIVE PRO FOOTBALL

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

The short version

Pick the Play only keeps what it needs to run the game: the username you choose, a random sign-in code, and your picks, points and lounges. No email, no phone number, no location, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared. You can delete your account at any time.

What we store

  • Your username. The name you pick when you start playing. You don't give us your real name, email address or phone number, and there is no password.
  • A sign-in token. A random code created when you sign up. It is kept on your device and on our server so the app can recognize you. It contains nothing about you.
  • Your game activity. Each pick you make (run or pass, direction and distance) and when you made it, the points you earn, your season total, and your positions on the leaderboards.
  • Lounges. The names of lounges you create and which lounges you have joined.

Your username, points and leaderboard positions are visible to other players, and lounge names and members are visible to the people in that lounge.

What we don't collect

Pick the Play does not collect your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. It contains no advertising, no analytics or third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

How your data is used

Only to run the game: to save your picks, score them, and show the live and lounge leaderboards. We do not sell your data, share it with anyone, or use it for advertising.

Like any online service, our server and hosting provider keep standard technical logs, which may include your IP address, to operate the service and keep it secure.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your account until you delete it, or until the game operator resets the game database.

To delete your account in the iPhone app, go to Settings > Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your username, sign-in token, picks and points, your lounge memberships, and any lounges you host. Your username becomes available for others to use. If you play in a web browser instead of the iPhone app, ask the person running your game to remove your account.

Children

Pick the Play is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
